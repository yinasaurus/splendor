                Goal Your turn Cards This screen Nobles Winning Tips

                🏆 The Goal: 15 Prestige Points

                The game enters its Final Round as soon as any player reaches 15 Prestige Points.

                • Fair Play: The round continues until everyone has had an equal number of turns.
                • The Winner: The player with the highest score wins.
                • Tie-Breaker: If scores are tied, the player who purchased fewer development cards wins.

                🕹️ Your Turn (Choose ONE Action)

                1. Take 3 Different Gems
                  • Pick 3 gems of different colors (excluding Gold).
                  • Your inventory cannot exceed 10 gems (including Gold) at end of turn.
                  • If you have more than 10 gems, you must return tokens until you have 10.
                2. Take 2 Identical Gems
                  • Pick 2 gems of the same color.
                  • Requirement: only available if there are 4 or more gems of that color left in the stack.
                3. Reserve a Card (+1 Gold)
                  • Choose a card from the market (or top of a deck) to your private Reserved hand (max 3).
                  • You gain 1 Gold Token (acts as a Wild/Joker).
                  • The market slot is immediately replenished from the deck.
                  • If no gold is left, you can still reserve but you do not gain gold.
                4. Purchase a Card
                  • Buy from the market or from your reserved hand.
                  • The market slot is immediately replenished.

                💎 The Power of Bonuses (Discounts)

                • Every card you buy stays in your engine and provides a permanent bonus.
                • Automatic Discount: each bonus icon counts as 1 gem of that color for future purchases.
                • Free Buys: if your bonuses match/exceed a card cost, you can purchase without spending tokens.
                • Stacking: bonuses and tokens combine.
                • Example: cost is 3 Blue and you have 2 Blue bonuses, so you only need 1 Blue token.

                Reading this app

                • Board gems — supply you take from.
                • Level 1–3 headers — include how many cards are left in that level’s deck (face-down pile).
                • Card cost — gems (and gold) to pay.
                • Card bonus — discount you keep after purchase.
                • Players — your gems, bonuses, bought & reserved cards.
                • Nobles — need bonuses (not loose gems) to visit you.

                👑 Noble Visitors

                Nobles are worth 3 points each and appear at the top of the screen.

                • Automatic Check: the game checks Noble visits at the end of every turn.
                • Requirements: you must have the required number of permanent card bonuses (not tokens).
                • Choosing a Noble: if you qualify for more than one, you choose which one to receive.

                End of game

                After someone hits 15 prestige, the final round runs so each other player gets one more turn. Then the leaderboard decides the winner.

                Tips

                • Early level-1 buys = strong discounts later.
                • Nobles are free points once your bonuses line up.
