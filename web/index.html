                How to play

                One action per turn. Use the board on the left and your player card on the right. This window scrolls — jump with the links below.

                Goal Your turn Cards This screen Nobles Winning Tips

                Goal

                Reach 15 prestige (default) first — but when someone does, everyone else still gets one last turn. Highest score wins; ties go to whoever bought fewer development cards.

                Your turn — pick exactly one

                1. Take gems
                  • 3 different colors, or
                  • 2 of the same color only if at least 4 of that color are on the board.
                  • You can’t take gold this way. Max 10 gems in hand.
                2. Reserve a card (max 3 reserved)
                  • Take a face-up card into your reserve.
                  • Gain 1 gold chip if any are left.
                3. Buy a card
                  • Face-up on the board, or one of your reserved cards (button on your player panel).
                  • Pay with gems in hand plus bonuses from cards you already bought. Gold counts as any color.

                Cards & bonuses

                • Level 1 — cheap, often 0 points, builds discounts.
                • Level 2 — medium cost, often 1–2 prestige.
                • Level 3 — expensive, big prestige.
                • Each card’s bonus color is a permanent discount on future buys of that gem color.

                Reading this app

                • Board gems — supply you take from.
                • Level 1–3 headers — include how many cards are left in that level’s deck (face-down pile).
                • Card cost — gems (and gold) to pay.
                • Card bonus — discount you keep after purchase.
                • Players — your gems, bonuses, bought & reserved cards.
                • Nobles — need bonuses (not loose gems) to visit you.

                Nobles

                At the end of your turn, if your bonuses match a noble, they visit automatically and grant extra prestige. You never pay gems for them.

                Noble names here are just labels (e.g. “Noble 1”). What matters is the gem requirements and prestige shown on the tile.

                End of game

                After someone hits 15 prestige, the final round runs so each other player gets one more turn. Then the leaderboard decides the winner.

                Tips

                • Early level-1 buys = strong discounts later.
                • Nobles are free points once your bonuses line up.
