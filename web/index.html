    How to Play Splendor

    Goal: Be the first player to reach the required prestige points (default 15) to win.

    On your turn, choose ONE action:

    1. Take gems
      • Option A: Take 3 different colored gems (e.g., R, E, S).
      • Option B: Take 2 of the same color (only if there are 4+ of that color on the board).
      • You cannot take GOLD directly and you cannot exceed 10 total gems.
    2. Reserve a card
      • Reserve a face-up card from the board (max 3 reserved cards).
      • Gain 1 GOLD (wild) gem if any are available.
    3. Purchase a card
      • Buy a face-up card or one of your reserved cards.
      • Pay using gems in hand plus permanent color bonuses from cards you already own.
      • GOLD gems can substitute any color.

    Cards and bonuses

    • Level 1 cards: cheap, usually 0 points, but give permanent color bonuses (discounts).
    • Level 2 cards: mid-cost, often 1–2 prestige.
    • Level 3 cards: expensive, give lots of prestige.
    • Each purchased card’s color gives you a permanent discount of that color for future cards.

    What “discount” means (UI)

    • On each card, the bonus is the discount color you get after buying it.
    • Your player bonuses (in the Players panel) are permanent discounts that apply to ALL future purchases of that gem color.
    • A noble’s requirements look at bonuses, not loose gems.

    How to read the screen

    • Board Gems: the tokens you can take from the bank.
    • Card cost: how many gems (or wild/gold substitutions) you must pay to buy it.
    • Card bonus: the permanent discount color you gain after purchase.
    • Players → gems: loose gems you currently hold.
    • Players → bonuses: permanent discounts you earned.
    • Nobles: requirements for bonuses (free extra prestige).

    Nobles

    • Nobles show color requirements in permanent bonuses (not loose gems).
    • At the end of your turn, if your bonuses meet a noble’s requirement, that noble visits you automatically and gives extra points.

    Winning & tie-breaker

    • When a player reaches the winning points, finish the round so everyone has played the same number of turns.
    • Highest prestige wins; if tied, the player with fewer purchased cards wins.

    Tips

    • Buy cheap Level 1 cards early to build strong discounts.
    • Plan towards nobles—they are free extra points once your bonuses match.
